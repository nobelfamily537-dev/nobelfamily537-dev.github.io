About AniPix

A free anime wallpaper app for Android — and a small independent publishing project behind it.

Home › About

AniPix is a free anime wallpaper app for Android, and a small independent publishing project built around it. There is no company behind it, no investors and no team — it is made and maintained by one anime fan who got tired of wallpaper apps that were slow, cluttered with locked content, or wanted a monthly subscription for the good stuff.

Why AniPix exists

Most wallpaper apps on Android fall into one of two categories. Either they are enormous and full of ads, or they are polished and expect you to pay for anything worth looking at. AniPix was built to be neither. The idea was simple: a wallpaper app that looks like it was made by someone who actually watches anime, that gives you the good wallpapers for free, and that does not waste your time.

That is why the app ships with no subscription, no locked premium shelf, and no requirement to create an account before you can browse. You install it, you open it, and the wallpapers are there.

What is inside the app

AniPix carries more than 2,400 HD wallpapers across eight categories: anime, games, gods and mythology, nature, space, and AI art, alongside animated live wallpapers. Every image is cropped to 1080×1920 and optimised for phone screens, so nothing looks stretched or blurry when you set it.

Beyond the wallpapers themselves, the app includes a search that understands aliases and forgives typos, a daily hand-picked “Today’s pick”, favourites, comments and reactions, and an in-app chat where users can talk about what they are downloading. Profiles, coins, streaks and donor badges are there for people who want them; if you would rather just browse and set wallpapers, you never have to sign in at all.

What we publish outside the app

The app is only part of the project. Two free publications run alongside it:

  • AniPix Blog (blog.anipix.me) — episode-by-episode anime guides, reviews, watch lists and release dates. Long-form, written by hand, no clickbait.
  • Orbit (space.anipix.me) — India’s space missions and the science of the universe, explained in plain language in both English and Hindi, illustrated with real ISRO and NASA photography.

Everything on both sites is free to read. There is no paywall, no login and no newsletter wall.

How we work

Articles and guides are written by us in English, and the Orbit site is also published in Hindi. Space imagery comes from public-domain sources such as NASA, ISRO and Wikimedia Commons, and is credited wherever it appears. Anime artwork shown inside the app belongs to its original creators, and if you own an image and want it removed, a single email is enough — it will be taken down.

We do not use auto-generated content, we do not scrape other sites, and we do not publish articles we have not actually researched. When a detail cannot be confirmed — an unannounced episode count, an unconfirmed release date — we say so rather than guessing.

How AniPix is funded

AniPix is free and always will be. The app and both websites are supported by advertising. That is the entire business model: no subscriptions, no paid tiers, no selling user data. Ads keep the wallpapers free and the writing free, and that is the trade.

Who runs it

AniPix is an independent project. It is not affiliated with any anime studio, publisher, app store, or game developer, and it does not claim to be. All characters, titles and artwork referenced belong to their respective rights holders.
