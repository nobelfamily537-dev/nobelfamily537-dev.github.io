AniPix FAQ: Downloading, Installing and Using the App

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Short, direct answers to the questions that come up most often about AniPix and about wallpapers in general. If your question is not here, the contact page is the fastest route.

About the app

Is AniPix free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, and it is supported by advertising. There is no paywall on the wallpaper library.

Where do I download it?

From the download link on this site. The app is distributed as a direct APK rather than through the Play Store, so your phone will ask you to allow installation from your browser the first time. That prompt is normal for any app installed outside a store.

Why is it not on the Play Store?

Publishing on the Play Store requires a developer account, a review process and ongoing compliance work. The app is distributed directly so that updates can ship the same day they are finished.

Is it safe to install?

The APK is built from this project's own source and served from this project's own release page. You can verify the download URL before installing — it should point at the project's GitHub releases. Do not install a copy from a third-party APK mirror; those are repackaged by whoever runs the mirror and are not under our control.

Does it work offline?

Wallpapers you have already viewed are cached, and wallpapers you have downloaded are stored locally. Browsing new wallpapers needs a connection.

How often are new wallpapers added?

Several times a day. The library is updated on a schedule and the app picks up new additions automatically when it refreshes.

Using wallpapers

Why does my wallpaper look blurry?

Almost always because the source image is smaller than your screen. Check the actual pixel dimensions, not the filename. A 720-pixel-wide image on a 1080-pixel screen will look soft no matter how good it looked as a thumbnail.

Why is my wallpaper cropped?

Your screen is taller than most wallpaper images. A standard image is 9:16; most phones are close to 9:20. Something has to be cut. Cropping the image yourself before setting it gives you control over what is lost.

Can I use the same wallpaper on both screens?

Yes, but set them separately. On most Android phones, setting one image for both means the lock screen gets a crop of the home screen's crop, which often cuts the subject.

Do dark wallpapers save battery?

On OLED screens, yes — meaningfully, especially at high brightness. On LCD screens, no. The saving depends on how much of the image is genuinely black rather than merely dark.

Live wallpapers

My live wallpaper does not animate. Why?

Three common causes: the system applied the app's static preview instead of the live service, the app is missing the "display over other apps" permission, or battery optimisation suspended it. Setting the wallpaper from inside the app fixes the first; the other two are in system settings.

Do live wallpapers drain battery?

Yes, more than static ones. A looping video is a running video decoder. The cost is manageable if you keep the loop short and the resolution modest.

Content and rights

Can I use these wallpapers anywhere I like?

The app provides wallpapers for personal use on your own devices. Artwork depicting anime and game characters belongs to the studios and artists who made it, and is provided here for personal, non-commercial use. Do not resell it, and do not use it in commercial products.

How do I request removal of an image?

Use the contact page with the image description and a link. Rights requests are handled directly and the image is removed when a claim is valid.

Technical

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8 and later. Live wallpapers need Android 8 or later as well; some animation features depend on the manufacturer's launcher.

The app will not install. What now?

Check that "install unknown apps" is enabled for the browser or file manager you are using to open the APK. On some phones this permission is per-app rather than global, so enabling it once for your browser is not enough if you then open the file from a file manager.
