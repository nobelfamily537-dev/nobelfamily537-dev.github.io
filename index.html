Questions

Quick answers.

Is AniPix free?

Yes. AniPix is completely free and supported by short ads. There is no subscription and no Play Store purchase.

Do I need to sign in?

No — you can browse and set wallpapers without signing in. Sign in with Google only if you want coins, favourites sync, comments or chat.

Where do the wallpapers come from?

New wallpapers are added every day from public anime art collections. If you own an image and want it removed, just contact us.

Why is it not on the Play Store?

AniPix is a small indie project, so it ships as a direct APK. That also means updates reach you faster.
