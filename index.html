About

What is AniPix?

AniPix is a free anime wallpaper app for Android, built by an anime fan who got tired of wallpaper apps that were slow, cluttered with locked content, or wanted a monthly subscription for the good stuff. It started as a small personal project and grew into a library of more than 2,400 hand-picked wallpapers across eight categories.

Every wallpaper in AniPix is cropped to 1080×1920 and optimised for phone screens, so nothing looks stretched or blurry once you set it. Alongside static images there are animated live wallpapers — real video wallpapers that keep playing behind your apps instead of sitting frozen. New wallpapers are added daily, and a hand-picked “Today’s pick” sits at the top of the app every morning.

Search is where AniPix stands out. Instead of forcing you to spell a character’s name exactly right, it understands aliases and forgives typos — type jjk and you get Jujutsu Kaisen; type itatchi and Itachi’s wallpapers show up anyway. That sounds small until you use an app that does not do it.

AniPix is also free in the real sense: no subscription, no locked premium shelf, no Play Store account required. It ships as a direct APK, which means updates reach you in minutes rather than after a store review. The app is supported by short ads, and that is the whole business model.

There is a community side too. You can sign in with Google to save favourites, leave comments, react to wallpapers and chat with other fans — with usernames, badges, coins and streaks. If you would rather just browse and set wallpapers, you never have to sign in at all.

Beyond the app, AniPix publishes free anime writing on the AniPix Blog — episode-by-episode guides, reviews, watch lists and release dates — and runs Orbit, a space-science site in English and Hindi covering India’s space missions with real ISRO and NASA photography. Both are free and open to everyone.

FAQ

Questions

Is AniPix really free?

Yes. AniPix is completely free and supported by short ads. There is no subscription, no purchase and no Play Store requirement.

Do I have to sign in?

No. You can browse and set wallpapers without signing in. Sign in with Google only if you want coins, favourites sync, comments or chat.

Where do the wallpapers come from?

New wallpapers are added daily from public anime art collections, then cropped and optimised for phones. If you own an image and want it removed, just contact us and it will be taken down.

Why isn’t it on the Play Store?

AniPix is an independent, teen-built project, so it ships as a direct APK. That also means updates reach you faster than a store review would allow.

Is it safe to install the APK?

Yes. The APK is built and signed by the AniPix developer and served from the official GitHub release page. Android will ask you to allow installs from this source — that is normal for any app installed outside the Play Store.

How do I update AniPix?

Open the app and you will be told when a new version is out. Download the new APK and install it over the old one — your favourites and settings stay.

Will it work on my phone?

AniPix needs Android 7.0 or newer. Live wallpapers work on most modern devices; everything else runs everywhere.

Does it use a lot of battery or data?

Static wallpapers use essentially nothing. Live wallpapers use more battery, as any animated wallpaper does, so use them if your phone handles it well. Wallpapers are compressed, so browsing uses very little data.

Can I request a wallpaper?

Yes — ask in the in-app chat or message us on Telegram, and we will try to add it. Requests for popular characters usually show up within a few days.

Do you have an iPhone version?

Not yet. AniPix is Android-only for now. The wallpapers themselves are free to browse on this site and on our blog.
